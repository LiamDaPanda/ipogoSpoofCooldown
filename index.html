Spoofer rules of thumb
  • Wait the full cooldown. Any interaction — spinning stops, throwing balls, feeding berries, joining a raid — before it expires escalates the soft ban.
  • The 8-minute rule. Once the cooldown is 8 minutes or longer, the community rule is always sit it out. Trying to shave a few seconds off in this window is the classic soft-ban trigger.
  • Add a 20–30 s buffer after landing. Give the game time to load the new area before you interact — that's built into the "Worth it?" check above.
  • Two-hour cap. Anything over ~1500 km hits the max 2-hour cooldown — same wait whether it's 1,500 km or the far side of the planet.
  • Despawn window. Wild Pokémon last up to 30 min from spawn. If your cooldown plus buffer eats most of that, the encounter's already gone.
